About Vastra Sundari

The Art of Traditional Elegance

“Every Chaniya Choli carries the colours, stories and celebrations of Gujarat.”

Vastra Sundari is devoted to one craft alone — the Chaniya Choli. From the swirling garba nights of Navratri to the sacred moments of a wedding, each piece is created to honour Gujarat's royal heritage.

We work with skilled artisans whose hands keep alive the traditions of Kutchi mirror work, bandhani and intricate hand embroidery, bringing them together with refined, contemporary silhouettes.
